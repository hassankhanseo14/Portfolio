AEO & GEO

Search Beyond Google

People now get answers from AI assistants and generative search as well as the ten blue links. I prepare your site for those journeys without ignoring the fundamentals.

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

Concise, well-structured answers, FAQ and schema markup, so your content is easy to extract for answer boxes and voice results.

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

Clear, well-sourced, expert content that generative search tools can understand and reference accurately.

AI Search Visibility & Entity Optimization

Consistent entity signals across your site, profiles and structured data, so search systems understand who you are and what you offer.

One connected strategy. Multiple search journeys. A stronger foundation for discoverability.

AI search platforms are not controllable. The goal is better foundations, not guaranteed citations.
